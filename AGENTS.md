# App conventions

- Keep all user-facing app copy in English, including alerts, empty states, accessibility labels, and permission descriptions. Official service names retain their verified spelling.
- Reuse the existing Unbounded headings, rounded system body font, design tokens, and native glass controls.
- Keep safety support compact, with clear hierarchy and a coral emergency action. Select contacts automatically from the device region; do not add a country picker, manual region override, or location permission.
- Contact numbers and service hours must come from the verified offline catalog. Unknown hours remain unknown.
